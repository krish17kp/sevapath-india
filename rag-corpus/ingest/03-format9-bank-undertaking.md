## What Format 9 is

Format 9 is an undertaking addressed to the branch manager of the bank that will
pay the pension. Its header cites the rules it is made under. [FORMAT9: Format 9,
page 1 header — "(See Rules 57, 58, 60, 63, 71, 74, 76, 79 and 80)"]

In it, the signatory agrees to refund or make good any amount to which they are
not entitled, or any amount credited to their account in excess of what they
would be entitled to, and agrees to indemnify the bank against loss arising from
crediting the pension to that account. [FORMAT9: Format 9, page 1, body
paragraph 1]

Format 9 also has a line recording the date of birth of the spouse and a mark of
identification. [FORMAT9: Format 9, page 1, paragraph 2]

## Why it is required

Rule 79(2)(a)(ii) names the undertaking to the bank in Format 9 as one of the
three things that accompany a Form 12 claim, alongside the claim itself and a
copy of the death certificate. [RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF
page 56]

Format 9 is required on the Form 10 route as well, so it is needed whichever
route applies. [RULE79: Rule 79(3)(iii), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

## What it does not do

<!-- product-policy: describes SevaPath behaviour, not a claim about official sources -->

Format 9 is an undertaking about repayment. Signing it does not establish
eligibility for family pension and does not fix any amount. SevaPath does not
compute or estimate any amount.
