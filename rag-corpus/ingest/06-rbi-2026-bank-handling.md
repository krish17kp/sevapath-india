## Which document this is

These are the Reserve Bank of India directions to agency banks on disbursing
Government pension, issued as RBI/DGBA/2026-27/399, reference
CO.DGBA.GBD.No.S43/31.02.007/2026-27, dated 30 April 2026 and updated as on
24 June 2026. They apply to the banks authorised to disburse Central and State
Government pension. [RBI2026: Chapter I, paragraphs 1 and 3]

## The bank must not insist on a new account

Agency banks shall not insist on opening a new account for credit of family
pension after the death of the pensioner, if the spouse as family pensioner opts
for an existing joint account for credit of family pension. [RBI2026: Chapter
VII(E), paragraph 23]

This is useful to know before visiting the branch: if the pension was already
being credited to a joint account, the surviving spouse can ask for family
pension to be credited to that same account.

## PPO number on the passbook

Agency banks shall record the PPO number in all pension passbooks of pensioners
and family pensioners, and the direction names commencement of family pension to
a spouse after the death of the pensioner as one of the situations this is meant
to help with. [RBI2026: Chapter VII(F), paragraph 24]

## Recovery of excess payment

The Directions say banks may ensure that no recovery of an excess amount is effected without the
pensioner's knowledge and consent, or without a prior notice, and any such
recovery must conform to the applicable service rules. [RBI2026: Chapter III,
opening paragraph]

Where excess or wrongful payment arose from bank-attributed errors such as
clerical errors, arithmetical miscalculations or incorrect application of
instructions, the entire amount is to be credited to the Government account in
lump sum immediately on detection. [RBI2026: Chapter III(A), paragraph 8(i)]

This is the backdrop to the Format 9 undertaking, which is the claimant's side of
the same subject.

## Service expectations at the branch

Agency banks are directed to appoint nodal officers for pension grievances at
each region or zone, and to have designated nodal officers for pension related
complaints outside the Centralised Pension Processing Centres, together with a
toll-free dedicated pension line. [RBI2026: Chapter VII(D), paragraphs 21 and 22]
