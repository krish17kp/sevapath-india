## When the Head of Office route applies instead

Form 10 is the application made to the Head of Office for family pension. Its
header cites several rules including rule 79. [FORM10: Form 10, page 1 header —
"[See rules 50, 71, 74, 76, 79 and 80]"]

The rule sends the claim to the Head of Office in two situations: where the
Pension Payment Order does not include the name of any member of the family for
payment of family pension, and where the Head of Office is of the opinion that
family pension has become payable to a member of the family other than the one
named in the Pension Payment Order. [RULE79: Rule 79(2)(b)(i), printed page 177,
PDF page 56]

This is why SevaPath treats Form 10 as the alternative route rather than the
default. A spouse already named in the Pension Payment Order normally uses the
Form 12 route to the Pension Disbursing Authority.

## What the Head of Office does

On a claim in Form 10, the Head of Office is directed to sanction the family
pension in Format 13, within one month of the date of receipt of the claim.
[RULE79: Rule 79(2)(b)(i), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

Form 10 is also the route where a pensioner or family pensioner has gone missing,
where a remarried widow or widower claims on behalf of minor children, where a
guardian claims for a minor or a person with a mental disability, and where a
Government servant died after retirement without the rule 57 or rule 58 forms
having been submitted. [RULE79: Rule 79(3)(iii), 79(4)(a)(ii), 79(5) and
Rule 80(5)(a), printed pages 177–178, PDF pages 56–57]

## Documents listed on Form 10

Form 10 lists its own attachments, including proof of identity, proof of
relationship with the deceased, details of family in Form 4, and an undertaking
for refunding any excess payment made by the pension disbursing bank in Format 9.
[FORM10: Form 10, page 3, list items 2, 3, 5 and 6]

The list also requires a copy of the first page of the pass book showing name and
account number, and states that the name of the claimant in the form and in the
bank account should be the same. [FORM10: Form 10, page 3, list item 19]
