## Where Rule 79 sits

Rule 79 opens Chapter XII, headed "Sanction of family pension and residuary
gratuity in respect of deceased or missing pensioners or family pensioner". The
rule is titled "Sanction of family pension and residuary gratuity on the death or
disappearance of a pensioner or family pensioner". [RULE79: Chapter XII heading
and Rule 79 title, printed page 176, PDF page 55]

## The two branches of Rule 79(2)

Rule 79(2)(a) is the branch for a claimant whose name is already in the Pension
Payment Order. The Pension Disbursing Authority commences disbursement, as
authorised in the Pension Payment Order, on a claim in Form 12 with a copy of the
death certificate and an undertaking in Format 9, within one month of receipt.
[RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

Rule 79(2)(b) is the branch for a claimant whose name is not in the Pension
Payment Order, or where the Head of Office considers family pension payable to
someone other than the person named. The Head of Office sanctions family pension
in Format 13 within one month of receiving a claim in Form 10. [RULE79: Rule
79(2)(b)(i), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

The same sub-rule covers the case where family pension was already being paid to
a family pensioner who has since died or become ineligible, and a co-authorised
disabled child, dependent parent or disabled sibling named in the Pension Payment
Order then claims in Form 12. [RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(v), printed page 177, PDF
page 56]

## Adding names to the Pension Payment Order

Where the deceased pensioner is survived by a spouse together with permanently
disabled children, dependent parents or disabled siblings whose names were not
previously included in the Pension Payment Order, the Accounts Officer includes
their names on a written communication from the Head of Office, made on a request
from the spouse in Form 8. [RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(iv), printed page 177, PDF page
56]

## After sanction

On receiving a sanction under Rule 79, the Accounts Officer authorises payment
within one month of receipt of the sanction. [RULE79: Rule 80(1), printed page
178, PDF page 57]

The Accounts Officer forwards a copy of the Pension Payment Order together with
the Format 9 undertaking to the Central Pension Accounting Office within one
month, that office issues a Special Seal of Authority within ten days, and the
Pension Disbursing Authority acts to disburse within fifteen days of receiving
it. [RULE79: Rule 80(2), 80(3) and 80(4), printed page 178, PDF page 57]

## A limit stated in the rule itself

A claim is not to be rejected on the ground that the family member's details are
not available in Form 4 or in office records, if the Head of Office is otherwise
satisfied about eligibility. [RULE79: Rule 79(3)(vi), printed page 178, PDF page
57]

A member of the family other than the widow or widower must submit the documents
referred to in Rule 50(12)(b) along with the claim, in order to decide
eligibility. Deciding eligibility is the department's function, not SevaPath's.
[RULE79: Rule 79(3)(vii), printed page 178, PDF page 57]
