## The warning

Form 14 is listed under the "Archives" heading on the Pensioners' Portal forms
page, described as the form of application for the grant of family pension on the
death of a Government servant, pensioner or family pensioner. It is not in the
current forms list. [FORMSLIST: "Archives" section entry for Form 14]

For a spouse named in the Pension Payment Order, the current route is a claim in
Form 12 to the Pension Disbursing Authority under Rule 79(2)(a)(ii). [RULE79:
Rule 79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

## Why people are still told to use Form 14

The Department's Frequently Asked Questions document, which predates the Central
Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 2021, still answers the question "When does a
family member become eligible for the grant of family pension?" by directing, in
its own words, that "in case of death of a pensioner the wife/disabled child/
dependent parents/disabled siblings should apply in Form No. 14 to the pension
disbursing authority". [FAQ2018: FAQ answer 8.2, page 17]

That FAQ answer is superseded by the 2021 Rules and by the current forms list.
Where the FAQ and the 2021 Rules conflict, the 2021 Rules and the current forms
list govern.

## A related trap on the same page

The forms page also lists a *different* Form 12 under Archives — the old form of
application for the grant of death gratuity on the death of a Government servant.
The current Form 12 is the family pension commencement claim in the live forms
list, not the archived death gratuity form. [FORMSLIST: current forms list entry
for Form 12 and "Archives" section entry for the old Form 12]

## What SevaPath does with this
