## Amounts

SevaPath does not calculate, estimate, or quote a family pension amount, a rate,
a percentage, or arrears. The amount payable to a spouse named in the Pension
Payment Order is the amount indicated in that Pension Payment Order, and the
Pension Disbursing Authority disburses it as authorised there. [RULE79: Rule
79(2)(a)(i) and 79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

Anyone asking what they will receive should read the Pension Payment Order and
ask the Pension Disbursing Authority.

## Eligibility

SevaPath does not decide whether anyone is eligible for family pension.
Eligibility under the rules is determined by the department. The rules place that
task with the Head of Office and set out what a claimant other than a widow or
widower must submit in order for eligibility to be decided. [RULE79: Rule
79(3)(vii), printed page 178, PDF page 57]

## Identity

SevaPath does not certify identity, does not resolve a difference between two
differently written names, and does not collect Aadhaar, PAN, account numbers,
OTPs, passwords or payment details. The prototype uses synthetic records only.

## Submission

SevaPath does not submit anything to any government system. Its output is a
preparation summary the claimant carries to the Pension Disbursing Authority or
the Head of Office. Any submission and receipt shown in the prototype is a
demonstration.

## Standing of this guidance

The Pensioners' Portal states that the information on it aims to give a general
overview and is not a substitute for any rules, and advises consulting the
original rules and orders for more specific information. SevaPath links to the
originals for the same reason. [PORTALTERMS: Disclaimer]

The hackathon brief this prototype was built for requires the use of mock or
synthetic data wherever personal information, payments, OTPs or government
systems would normally be involved, and forbids presenting the prototype as an
official government product. [BRIEF: "What not to do"]

## The exact refusal wording

When the corpus does not support an answer, SevaPath replies exactly:
`I could not verify this from the current official corpus.`
