## The three things the rule names

Rule 79(2)(a)(ii) names the claim in Form 12, a copy of the death certificate of
the deceased pensioner, and an undertaking to the bank in Format 9. [RULE79: Rule
79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

## The list printed on Form 12 itself

Form 12 carries its own list of documents to be submitted with it. The items that
apply to a surviving spouse claiming in their own right are:

- Two specimen signatures of the claimant, furnished on a separate sheet. A
  person who is not literate enough to sign may furnish two slips each bearing
  left hand thumb and finger impressions. [FORM12: Form 12, page 2, list item 1]
- Two copies of passport size photographs of the claimant. [FORM12: Form 12, page
  2, list item 2]
- The undertaking for refunding any excess payment made by the pension disbursing
  bank. [FORM12: Form 12, page 2, list item 3]
- A copy of the Pension Payment Order of the pensioner or previous family
  pensioner, to be provided if available. [FORM12: Form 12, page 2, list item 7]
- A copy of the death certificate of the deceased pensioner or previous family
  pensioner. [FORM12: Form 12, page 2, list item 9]

## Items that apply only when there is a guardian or nominee

Items 4, 5, 6 and 8 of the same list — specimen signature of the guardian, two
self-attested photographs of the guardian or nominee, a descriptive roll of the
guardian or nominee, and proof of permanent address of the guardian — apply where
a guardian or nominee is involved, for example where the claimant is a minor or
is suffering from a disorder or disability of mind. [FORM12: Form 12, page 2,
list items 4, 5, 6 and 8; Form 12, page 1, item 3]

A surviving spouse claiming in their own right does not need the guardian items.

## Bank details asked on the form

Form 12 asks for the account number, the bank's name and branch, and the IFS
code of the account to which family pension is to be credited. [FORM12: Form 12,
page 1, item 4]

## A statement the claimant signs

Form 12 states that the claimant is aware that future good conduct of the
claimant or family pensioner shall be an implied condition for every grant of
family pension and its continuance. [FORM12: Form 12, page 1, declaration above
the signature block]
