## Who this route is for

Form 12 is the application a spouse or co-authorised family member submits to the
Pension Disbursing Authority to start family pension after a pensioner or family
pensioner dies. The form's own header carries this description and cites the rule
it is made under. [FORM12: Form 12, page 1 header — "[See Rule 79(2)]"]

The route applies when the claimant's name is already included in the Pension
Payment Order. The rule directs the Pension Disbursing Authority to begin paying
family pension, as authorised in the Pension Payment Order, to the widow or
widower whose name has been included in that order. [RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(ii),
printed page 177, PDF page 56]

## What the claimant sends, and to whom

The claim goes to the Pension Disbursing Authority — in most cases the pension
paying bank branch — not to the Head of Office. [FORM12: Form 12, page 1 header]

Rule 79(2)(a)(ii) names three things that accompany the claim: the claim in Form
12, a copy of the death certificate, and an undertaking to the bank in Format 9.
[RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

## Timing stated in the rule

The Pension Disbursing Authority is directed to commence disbursement within one
month of receiving the claim. [RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF
page 56]

Family pension becomes payable from the date following the date of death of the
pensioner. [RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(i), printed page 177, PDF page 56]

## Why this is the current form

The Pensioners' Portal forms page lists Form 12 with this description in its
current "Downloads: Application/Claim Forms" list, not under Archives.
[FORMSLIST: current forms list entry for Form 12]

## What Form 12 asks for

Form 12 asks for the name of the pensioner on whose death family pension is
claimed, the date of death, the PPO number, the claimant's name, date of birth,
relationship and postal address, and the bank account number, bank name and
branch, and IFS code to which family pension is to be credited. [FORM12: Form 12,
page 1, items 1, 2 and 4]

Providing a PAN is requested on the form and providing an Aadhaar number is
marked voluntary. SevaPath never collects either. [FORM12: Form 12, page 1,
footer fields]
