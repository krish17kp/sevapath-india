## The order SevaPath applies

1. The Central Civil Services (Pension) Rules, 2021 as notified in the Gazette.
   This is the primary law for the journey SevaPath covers. [RULE79: Rule 79,
   printed pages 176–178, PDF pages 55–57]
2. The current official form issued under those rules — Form 12, Form 10, Format
   9 — for what a claimant actually fills in and attaches.
3. The Pensioners' Portal current forms list, for deciding which form is current
   and which is archived. [FORMSLIST: current forms list and "Archives" section]
4. Reserve Bank of India directions, for what the bank must do on its side.
5. Departmental FAQs and general portal guidance, for background only.

## The known conflict in this corpus

The Department's FAQ document predates the 2021 Rules and still directs a
claimant to Form 14. [FAQ2018: FAQ answer 8.2, page 17] The 2021 Rules and the
current forms list direct a spouse named in the Pension Payment Order to Form 12.
[RULE79: Rule 79(2)(a)(ii), printed page 177, PDF page 56] [FORMSLIST: current
forms list entry for Form 12; "Archives" section entry for Form 14]

By the order above, the 2021 Rules and the current forms list govern, and the FAQ
answer is recorded only as superseded guidance so that SevaPath can warn about
it.

## Claims that are not in this corpus

Anything not supported by a source in this corpus is answered with:
`I could not verify this from the current official corpus.`
SevaPath does not fill a gap from general knowledge.
