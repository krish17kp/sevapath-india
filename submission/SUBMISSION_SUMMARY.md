# Submission summary

SevaPath helps a surviving spouse start preparing a Central Civil family-pension
claim after a pensioner dies. Today, the spouse may face older guidance that
mentions Form 14, current rules that distinguish Form 12 from Form 10, records
spread across a PPO, death certificate and bank proof, and mismatches discovered
only at the counter.

SevaPath turns that into one clear journey. Four plain-language questions select
the verified route. The prototype reads three visibly fictional records,
compares important fields without “fixing” them, flags unreadable information or
name differences for human review, explains whether Form 12 or Form 10 applies,
and produces a source-linked checklist and non-official preparation worksheet.
Its knowledge assistant answers only from a checked corpus of official rules,
forms and RBI material, cites every supported answer, and refuses to calculate
pension, determine eligibility or resolve identity.

The working build includes the complete supported journey, mismatch and missing-
evidence paths, route-specific worksheets, local retrieval, citations, mobile
and keyboard support, and an unmistakably labelled demonstration receipt.

All citizen records, submission behavior and receipts are mocked. SevaPath has
no upload, account, government or bank integration, makes no real submission,
and is not an official government service. Its advantage over a static
checklist is that it reacts safely to the citizen’s route and the evidence
actually present while preserving a human decision at every legal or identity
boundary.
